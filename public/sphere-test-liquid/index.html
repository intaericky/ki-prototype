Shell and water

The front quarter of the sphere is drawn from the viewer's eye, so from there the shell looks open and deep; the rest of the sphere stays a plain white shell. Water pours in from the top, rests, then drains through a whirlpool at the bottom. Viewer preview looks from that eye: drag to step away and see the trick bend, double-click to return.
