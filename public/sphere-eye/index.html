Every Earth-observation satellite, in real time. Each instrument lights the sphere only where it is looking at this instant, the way it actually looks: a scanner sweeps a line, a sounder clicks across in spots, a microwave radiometer rides an arc, radar watches a strip to one side, a laser leaves points, a geostationary imager rasters its disk. Small footprints are enlarged to the visible size you set; dim rings are satellites whose looking is tasked or unknown.
