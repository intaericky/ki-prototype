Live control
  1. On the computer that feeds the sphere, open the display link. On the tablet, open the tablet link. (Or open the pages and type the room code into their bar.)
  2. Press Start live control here. From then on every change you make in this studio (surface, frame, satellite, speed, and everything under Settings) appears on them within a moment.
  3. The displays keep the last settings even if they reload. Press Stop when you are done; they keep showing the last look.
