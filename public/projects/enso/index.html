How to Read This

OISST is distributed as a regular latitude/longitude grid, then sampled here onto geodesic sphere units. The units are mostly hexagons, with 12 pentagons required by spherical topology. Side faces connect neighboring units at their actual radii; boundary sides around removed land units fold inward toward the sphere center.

Land and missing ocean units are removed. Cool anomalies become shorter inward units rather than negative bars.

The long timeline uses a regional Niño 3.4 OISST anomaly subset from 1982-2025. The globe displays a high-resolution global frame for every month across the complete 1982-2025 series.
