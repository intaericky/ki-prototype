Sand and balls
Loading orbits…

The satellites NASA publishes daily or scene imagery from, each as a metal ball as wide as its swath, rolling on its real orbit (SGP4, CelesTrak elements) over an Earth-fixed soft sphere. Each ball rests on whatever sand is under it, old grooves included, and presses its own groove from there. The sand never resets; it only settles back by itself over the hours set in the panel. 360 frame lays the whole sphere out as one 1920 × 1080 equirectangular frame for the LED sphere; Open display shows only that, F for full screen.
